Privacy

CGN Invoicing is a private tool used by one tutoring business. It has a single user: the owner of that business.

What it accesses

With the owner's authorization, the tool reads and updates the owner's own QuickBooks Online company: invoices, customers, recurring invoice templates, payments and accounts-receivable reports.

Where data is kept

Data read from QuickBooks stays on the owner's own computer and in the owner's own Intuit and Google accounts. The tool has no server and no database of its own.

Sharing

Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. No one other than the owner has access.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect the tool at any time from the list of connected apps in QuickBooks. After that the tool can no longer read or update the company.
