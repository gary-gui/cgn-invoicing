CGN Invoicing

CGN Invoicing is a private tool used by one tutoring business to prepare its own monthly invoices in its own QuickBooks Online company.

It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up. Only the owner of the business can connect it, and it connects only to the owner's own QuickBooks company.

To connect or reconnect, the owner authorizes the tool from their own computer. To disconnect, the owner removes it from the list of connected apps in QuickBooks.
